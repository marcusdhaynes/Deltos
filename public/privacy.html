Deltos — Privacy Notice

Beta version · Updated October 17, 2026

What Deltos accesses

After you authorize Google access, Deltos retrieves Gmail messages, message contents, labels, sender details, and Gmail rules as needed to display and organize your mailbox. It may send mail or modify Gmail labels, drafts, rules, and messages when you use those features. You can use the demo without connecting Gmail.

Where information is handled

In browser-only mode, Deltos accesses the Gmail API from your browser, stores access-token and application state in browser storage, and does not deliberately send your messages through a Deltos-owned email-processing server. Your browser may retain local reminders, draft content, activity/undo history, and app settings. Clear site data in your browser to remove locally stored state.

If the optional persistent-login backend is enabled, it stores your Google account email address, encrypted access and refresh tokens, a hash of a random session handle, and timestamps in a database. Gmail content is still requested directly from Google by your browser. The backend uses session records to renew permission while authorized; sessions expire after 90 days without use unless Google access is revoked sooner. Backend account metadata and expired session records may persist until deleted by a cleanup process.

Images and external links

HTML emails are shown inside a restricted viewer. External images are blocked until you select “Show images.” If you load remote images or open email links, the external sites may collect your IP address and other request details. Gmail and Google identity services are subject to Google's policies.

Your controls

You can disconnect Gmail in Deltos. When a persistent session is active, disconnection requests deletion of that session; you can also revoke the app's Google permissions in your Google Account security settings. Deleting browser site data removes Deltos's local application state. Revoking access does not undo changes previously made in Gmail.

Security and limitations

Deltos requests Google permission before accessing Gmail. It uses HTTPS in deployment and encrypts refresh credentials on the optional backend. Browser-stored tokens and local message data are accessible to code running under Deltos's origin, so avoid using shared devices. Beta features such as scheduled sending or reminders may only operate while Deltos is open. No software can guarantee error-free operation; test destructive features with nonessential messages.

Contact

For product and privacy requests, use the Deltos project's issue tracker. Do not post private messages, tokens, account numbers, or sensitive personal information in public issues.
